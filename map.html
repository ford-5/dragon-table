🗺️ Battle Map

To show this map to your players, open the GM Screen, start a live game, and press "Show the map to players".

Place heroes, monsters and terrain on a grid. Each square is 5 feet. Drag tokens to move them: the map counts the movement by the rules.

📦 Pre-made maps & dungeons

Pick one to load it, with its monsters and your heroes at the start. You can change anything afterwards. Loading replaces the map you have now.

💾 My saved maps

Made something you like? Save it here (terrain, monsters, heroes and fog) and load it any time. Saved maps with monsters can also be played in Solo Adventures. They're kept in this browser.
