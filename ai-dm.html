💬 Your messages, your campaign choices and your hero's sheet (name, class, stats and item) are sent to Anthropic's Claude to write the Game Master's replies. Using the AI costs money on the account that owns the API key.

🧭 How to play

1. Read what the Game Master describes. 2. Type (or 🎤 say) what your hero does, in your own words, like "I sneak up to the door and listen", then press Send.

3. When something is risky, the GM asks for a roll and a big button appears. Click it: it rolls a d20 and adds your bonus for you. Higher is better!

4. In a fight, everyone takes turns. On your turn you can move and do one main thing, such as attack or cast a spell. Your Armor Class (AC) is how hard you are to hit, and Hit Points (HP) are how much damage you can take.

Stuck? Just ask the GM "What can I do?" They'll explain.
