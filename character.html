      ⚔️ Weapons & Armor

        Attacks

          Attack = d20 + the bonus shown. Melee weapons use Strength, ranged use Dexterity, and Finesse weapons use whichever is better. You add your proficiency bonus only for weapons your class is trained with.

          ✨ Spellcasting

            Spell slots

            Tap a ● to spend a slot, tap a ○ to get it back.

            Spells come from the free SRD. Damage and healing dice are read from each spell's text, so the description is the final word. Not tracked: a Wizard's spellbook, spells from species or feats, always-prepared spells, and a Warlock's Mystic Arcanum.
