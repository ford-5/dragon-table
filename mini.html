🖨️ Mini Maker

Turn your hero into a 3D-printable miniature

1. Whose mini?

Picking a hero fills these in. You can also choose any species and class by hand, for example to print a mini from a shop order.

2. Take a look

A simple, chunky figure built from basic shapes: species changes the build and features, class changes the gear. Two-class heroes use their first class's gear.

3. Print it

🖨️ How to print it

1. Open the .stl file in your printer's own software (the "slicer"): for example Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura or Orca Slicer. These came with your printer or are free to download.

2. Turn on supports ("tree" or "organic" supports if you have them). The figure is designed to need very few, but heads and arms are small overhangs.

3. Use fine settings: 0.08 to 0.12 mm layer height. A resin printer gives the most detail. If small parts don't come out well, tick "1.5 times bigger".

4. The model is made of overlapping shapes; slicers join them automatically. If yours says the model has errors, let it "repair" or "fix" the model.
