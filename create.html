🛠️ Create

Make your own maps and battles and save them for later, or start from ready-made ones. Everything is kept in this browser.

🗺️ Maps & dungeons

✏️ Draw a new map Walls, trees, water, monsters, heroes and fog of war. Then press 💾 Save this map. 📦 Pre-made maps Ready-made maps and dungeons to load and change. 💾 Your saved maps Maps you saved on the Battle Map page.

⚔️ Battles

A battle is a set of monsters. Open one in the Battle Simulator to see how hard it is, or put it straight on the Battle Map.

✏️ Build a new battle Pick monsters, check the difficulty by the rules, then press 💾 Save this battle.

🧙 Ready-made heroes

A finished character sheet for every class, built by the rules. Pick a level, then add one to your characters (for yourself, a friend, or the GM's helpers).
