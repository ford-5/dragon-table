1. The heroes

Tick who fights. Heroes come from your saved characters and use their best weapon from the sheet's Weapons & Armor (or a simple melee attack if they have none). You can change the numbers here.

2. The monsters

All of the SRD's monsters and animals. Search by name.

    3. How hard is it?

    From the SRD's XP Budget table. A single monster is usually Low difficulty for a party whose level equals its Challenge Rating.

    4. Fight!

    This is a simplified simulation to help you plan: heroes and monsters just attack (heroes pick the weakest foe, monsters pick a random hero). Spells, special abilities, breath weapons, healing, terrain and tactics are NOT simulated, and a hero at 0 HP is simply out. A real table is smarter than this, so treat the result as a guide, not a promise.
